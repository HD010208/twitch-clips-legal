# Privacy Policy - Twitch Clips

Last updated: 22 November 2026

## 1. Who we are
Twitch Clips is a small personal tool operated by a private individual. Contact: buisnessai0102@gmail.com

## 2. What data we process
When a TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, the service receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name and avatar) from TikTok. We also process the videos and captions that the account owner chooses to publish through the service.

## 3. Why we process it
We use this data only to publish the videos that the account owner selected to the connected TikTok account, and to show the connected account inside the tool. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 4. Storage and retention
Access tokens are stored securely and only for as long as the account stays connected. Videos are kept only as long as needed for publishing. You can disconnect at any time in your TikTok settings under security and permissions, and you can ask us to delete all stored data by e-mail.

## 5. Third parties
Publishing happens through the official TikTok API. TikTok processes data under its own privacy policy: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy

## 6. Your rights
You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your data at any time by e-mail to buisnessai0102@gmail.com.

## 7. Changes
This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
